BlazarLab privado 13.02 Lite
Project: independent experimental analysis site based on the approved public V7 Lite 1.3.51 visual foundation.
Purpose: local curve-of-light inspection, mapping, diagnostics, and experimental multiband calculations.
Privacy: no data upload endpoint; imported files remain in the local browser session. Exporting results is user-initiated.
Status: experimental research scaffold. Numerical agreement with independent implementations and validation against the intended real-data sets are still required before scientific publication.
Changes in 13.02: invalidate stale results after changing time/band mapping or analysis parameters; make delimiter detection quote-aware across sample rows; avoid mistaking semicolon-delimited rows for comments; clarify failed-import behavior and button wording.
Separation: do not publish this package to the public BlazarLab V7 repository. The public and private projects have separate versioning and storage keys.
Package: Lite site files only; no reports, screenshots, test scripts, or audit artefacts.
